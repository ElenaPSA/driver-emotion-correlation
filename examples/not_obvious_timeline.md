# Emotion-change timeline

This report identifies when the detected emotion label changed and lists driving signals that shifted in the adjacent matched samples.
A signal changing at the same time is a coincident association, not evidence that it caused or provoked the emotion change.

Signal shifts are described qualitatively after filtering out small changes relative to each signal's observed range in this session.
The analysis uses receipt-time buckets; it does not establish whether a signal changed before the emotional response.

## Limits

- The emotion is a detected label, not a direct measurement of the driver's internal state.
- Receipt-time matching can show co-occurrence, not causal order or what provoked a change.
- Session-level profiler summaries may be too coarse to explain second-by-second emotion changes.
- Use synchronized, short-window raw signals and controlled repeated observations to investigate cause.
